# A small demo: their main flow, in hours

For a meeting today or tomorrow, an early prospect, or thin evidence. Small is about how much is built. It is still
this client's demo, designed from what they said and from research, and it clears everything in `SKILL.md`.

The time goes to three things, in this order:

1. **The one flow that matters most to them**: the thing they complained about first or asked for by name, designed
   start to finish as they would live it: what arrives, who sees it, what they do, what changes.
2. **The first screen**: what this person needs in the morning, in their words, with their kind of customers,
   products and statuses on it. If only one screen gets real care, it is this one.
3. **One moment they can press**: the order, call or message arriving, and the number that moves because of it.

Two to four screens is usually right. Leave out anything that would be there only to make the demo look bigger.

Speed comes from parts and from plain files, not from another demo's screens: take the one or two parts this flow
needs (a seeded world and an incoming order, for example), dress them in this client's look, and write the rest. A
world of 20 to 60 rows that read as real, with a story (a busy Tuesday, one thing late).

When evidence is thin, say in one line what you assumed, and read their site and their trade for the rest. A real
term from their world on the first screen does more than three extra pages.

Done: the flow walked end to end as their persona on a phone; the first screen recognisably theirs; published.
